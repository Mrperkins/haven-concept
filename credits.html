Vehicle imagery

All HAVEN vehicle renders, concept sketches, interior illustrations and diagrams used on this site are exploratory AI-generated or digitally assembled concept assets developed during the HAVEN design discussions. No HAVEN production vehicle or functional prototype is depicted. Rendered labels, dimensions and mechanical systems must not be interpreted as verified specifications.

Design references

Visual direction studied SpaceX for cinematic full-bleed story sequencing, Lucid Motors for vehicle presentation, and Slate for owner customization philosophy. No third-party site code, trademark or copyrighted media has been reproduced.

Company & commercial status

HAVEN is an early-stage brand and automotive concept under feasibility study. The P1 $17,990 and P1R $129,990 delivered prices are aspirational targets, not verified selling prices. No supplier relationships, manufacturing program, capitalization, market launch, investor commitments or final vehicle engineering have been secured. Actual access to donor platforms and parts interchange remains unverified. The HAVEN name is pending trademark clearance.

Safety and ownership

Proposed owner-upgradeable technology is restricted to appropriately isolated and approved systems. Structural, powertrain, restraint, emissions and other safety-critical changes require engineering validation and regulatory compliance. Illustrative configurator prices are not quotations or offers.
