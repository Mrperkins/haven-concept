02 / Engineering rules

Protection is standard.
Promises require proof.

01 / SAFETY CELL

Strength in the structure.

The proposed integrated rollover/roof-ring protection belongs in the body engineering from the outset, with actual crash and roof-crush testing before performance claims.

02 / GUARDIAN

Protect the people inside.

Child-presence sensing, conventional car-seat anchors, warnings, monitored cabin conditions and backup escalation are high-priority design requirements—not substitutes for supervision.

03 / OPENARCHITECTURE

Designed to be kept.

Nonstructural skins, repairable trim and isolated computing may evolve. Occupant restraints and certified safety structures never become unvalidated plug-and-play upgrades.

The U.S. certification framework, crash-safety benchmark and independent safety case are unresolved. Heavier is not automatically safer; ballast or curb-weight increases solely for tax treatment are explicitly excluded.

03 / Phase 0

what can actually be built.
