## Introductory outreach text

**Subject:** HAVEN Motors — early-stage family SUV feasibility and platform scoping

Hello [Name],

I'm developing HAVEN, an early-stage U.S. family vehicle concept with two proposed SUVs: Core, targeted toward an accessible $35–40k price band, and Reserve, a premium companion with optional security features. Our differentiation hypothesis centers on independently validated family safety, serviceability, child-presence protection and optional modular comfort systems.

We are seeking a qualified automotive development partner for a **limited paid feasibility assessment** before committing to prototype tooling or production. The immediate questions are whether an existing licensable platform can support the proposed packaging and safety requirements, the main U.S. certification obstacles, a plausible path to supplier costing, and the scope/price of an initial technical gap review.

Would your team be open to a short introductory discussion or sending a menu of paid scoping services? I can provide a nonconfidential requirements summary; we would arrange an NDA before sharing nonpublic design or commercial details.

Thank you,
HAVEN concept team

## Questions to ask each firm

1. Do you accept concept-stage clients? What minimum spend and scope are required?
2. Can you examine **licensed OEM chassis/BIW rights** vs a rebody vs derivative, and advise what's commercially lawful?
3. What are the hardest engineering blockers for 5-person family packaging, roof-ring reinforcement and direct child-presence detection?
4. Is there a donor-based **static or private-road demonstrator** path that remains clearly non-production?
5. What data/licensing access is needed to retain braking, ADAS, crash management, restraint electronics and emissions?
6. Which powertrain architecture realistically supports a $35–40k eventual MSRP at modest volumes?
7. What are one-time NRE and recurring BOM cost bands? State years, quantities and exclusions.
8. Can the supplier responsibly deliver >6,000-lb GVWR while preserving safe payload and handling, **without unnecessary curb weight**?
9. Can potential ballistic side glazing be integrated and independently evaluated without invalidating vehicle crash performance?
10. What is the **cheapest informative next engineering study** you can actually deliver in 30 days?
11. What documents, CAD, models, supplier quotes and intellectual property rights would HAVEN receive at conclusion?
12. What assumptions would cause you to advise **not proceeding**?
