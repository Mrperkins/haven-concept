# HAVEN vs. China: competitive engineering benchmark (10 October 2026 evidence cut; compiled 11 October 2026)

**Status: Desk research only.** These are identified production, launched or advertised capabilities from manufacturer publications and dated reporting. No HAVEN system has been built or tested, and no Chinese OEM has agreed to any partnership. Where the manufacturer claims safety/performance, mark it as a **manufacturer claim**, not an independent result. Trim, software activation, region, charging equipment and product year all matter. Chinese domestic list prices do **not** convert directly to realistic U.S. MSRPs.

## Strategic rule

**HAVEN must win on family safety, ease of everyday use, reliability, repairability and competitive feature value — rather than seeking the longest possible gadget list.** Chinese vehicles already ship many of the novel ideas discussed for HAVEN: child-behavior awareness, AI occupant-directed climate, gesture-powered doors, multi-zone voice, motorized vehicle retrieval/parking, heated/cooled refrigerator compartments, 4-wheel steering and intelligent chassis. "We have a built-in cooler" is no longer a unique proposition.

### What HAVEN must NOT do

1. **Treat China launch sticker as a U.S. landed MSRP.** Retail prices reflect local scale, exchange rates, taxes, freight, tariffs, subsidies, financing, regulations and warranty.
2. **Claim superior safety based on roof strength or number of airbags alone.** Produce independent NHTSA/IIHS/Euro NCAP-equivalent applicable data before comparisons; broad "world's safest" is unsubstantiated.
3. **Pack every premium feature into the Core S.** Make explicit per-trim truth tables with safety essentials standard and a costed option package.
4. **Install unlicensed Chinese driver automation, telematics or connectivity software.** Investigate U.S. legal restrictions first.
5. **Marketing via unproven "autonomous" / flood rescue claims.** All Level 2 assist remains supervised, emergency floating features on Yangwang are specialty vendor claims and never justify public-road flood crossing.
6. **Let AI cloud outage disable HVAC, driver monitoring, crash egress or child alert functionality.** Emergency safety paths must have deterministic, monitored operation independent of chat.

## US legal sourcing: material gate, not just a supplier preference

The U.S. Bureau of Industry and Security's **January 2025 connected vehicles final rule** prohibits specified transactions involving certain connected vehicles, software and hardware with a sufficient China or Russia nexus. BIS summary states:

- **MY2027:** applicable connected-vehicle covered software and certain manufacturer-related transactions.
- **MY2030:** applicable vehicle connectivity hardware (or **Jan 1, 2029** for hardware not associated with a model year).
- Rule scoped to specified connected vehicles and systems; there are exemptions and authorization paths. **Do not infer every part made in China is forbidden**; review exact supplier, product system and control relationship with specialist counsel.
- China origin software development / supply arrangements may make a direct Chinese AI/ADAS partnership unsuitable even if mechanical parts or designs could be licensed.
- A separate federal **2026 Senate proposal** about broader restrictions is a **bill, not enacted law**; monitor its status rather than treating it as binding.

**Primary official source:** https://media.bis.gov/connected-vehicles ; https://media.bis.gov/press-release/commerce-finalizes-rule-secure-connected-vehicle-supply-chains-foreign-adversary-threats
