# HAVEN Motors | Program 0: evidence before tooling

**State as of 2026-10-11: CONCEPT / FEASIBILITY.** HAVEN has concept images and a website. It does **not** have a certified vehicle, vehicle CAD, contractual rights to an OEM platform, supplier quotations, reservations, a production facility, validated safety tests, or an agreed launch date.

## The two-product strategy

- **HAVEN Core SUV:** 5-seat family-first midsize SUV. Hypothetical U.S. MSRP $34,995 / $37,995 / $39,995. All fundamental safety systems, the integrated roof-ring Safety Cell, conventional FMVSS-compliant child-seat hardware and safeguards are part of the engineering baseline.
- **HAVEN Reserve SUV:** premium, potentially larger derivative. Hypothetical U.S. MSRP $54,995 / $64,995 / $74,995+ before customized security packages. Premium cabin, higher payload capacity where feasible, additional security compatibility; not a substitute for crash engineering.
- **P1 / P1R sedans:** remain design explorations. Not in the first SUV validation program. This is a prioritization decision, not cancellation.

**Brand promise:** safety first; useful family features; repairability and upgradeability that do not compromise certified safety; transparent ownership.

## A feasible technical entry point

1. Obtain a paid independent **vehicle feasibility / regulatory scoping review**.
2. Investigate *legally licensed* existing OEM architectures, contract engineering, and assembly partnerships. Do not imply that a purchasable donor vehicle gives HAVEN rights to commercialize a new rebodied production car.
3. Compare gasoline, HEV, PHEV and BEV implementations **after** exploring license, structure, software and safety dependencies. A common cabin/brand does not require a single universal underbody.
4. Develop dimensioned vehicle package / hardpoint targets, restraint layout and roof-ring requirements with a qualified automotive team.
5. Obtain qualified BOM and nonrecurring-engineering (NRE) estimates. Then choose proof-of-concept vehicle, private demonstrator or pause.

**Primary constraint:** the $35–40k Core price is a target, not cost substantiation. Start with safety essentials and low-cost family utility; move massage, costly tint, active refrigeration, and specialty armor to options / Reserve only if engineering and economics permit.

## Phase 0 — 90 days, user-approved budget $2,500–$10,000

| Window | Deliverable | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–7 | Product charter, interview guide, stakeholder matrix, RFI draft, competition shortlist, preliminary cost sensitivity | Documents and interview appointments |
| Days 8–30 | 30 semistructured family interviews; target 50+ independent survey answers; counsel-led name review | Evidence log, reasons-to-buy and reasons-not-to-buy |
| Days 15–60 | 3 qualified engineering contacts; platform/OEM rights discovery; initial regulatory gap analysis | Written notes, expressed capability, hard blockers |
| Days 31–75 | 1+ indicative price/scoping estimates **from qualified vendors**, packaging review, powertrain trade study | Supplier provenance, assumption revisions |
| Days 60–90 | Independent manufacturing feasibility memo, financing envelope and go/no-go decision | Stage-gate review signed off by founder and qualified advisers |

The targets are planning gates, not statistical proof of demand. A partner declining to license a platform is not overcome by making more renderings.

## Decision gates — ALL OPEN

- **G0 name/brand:** trademark clearance; no preemptive claim HAVEN is available.
- **G1 demand:** credible paid-customer value beyond aesthetics; capture willingness to trade feature X for price Y.
- **G2 platform:** written indication that platform licensing / engineering access is feasible; a retail donor is insufficient.
- **G3 safety:** qualified gap review for FMVSS and crash program.
- **G4 economics:** credible supplier cost ranges suggest any achievable Core specification inside $40k.
- **G5 launch path:** capital, certification, product liability, service and aftersales realistically funded.
- **G6 prototype:** only commit after previous gates; closed-course or static concept first.
