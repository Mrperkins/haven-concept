OPENING

BUILT TO EVOLVE.

HAVEN imagines desirable cars owners can maintain, personalize and upgrade over time.

02 / 11
THE PROBLEM

OWNERSHIP BECAME DISPOSABLE.

Locked-down electronics, expensive replacement parts, dated interiors and increasingly costly new vehicles create frustration for long-term owners.

03 / 11
OUR ANSWER

OPENARCHITECTURE.

Standardized service items, validated shared components, replaceable computing and modular exterior and interior upgrades.

04 / 11
P1

THE PEOPLE’S SPORTS SEDAN.

Four-door gasoline concept. Target delivered price $17,990, unverified. Security cameras, useful voice controls and customizable finishes are product requirements.

05 / 11
P1R

THE FOUNDER’S SERIES.

Distinctive $129,990-target performance grand tourer for halo positioning and engineering partnerships. Performance architecture and economics remain to be validated.

06 / 11
BUSINESS MODEL

A PLATFORM, NOT A ONE-TIME SALE.

Vehicle sales, carefully priced modular upgrades, parts interoperability and extended service partnerships, subject to legal and technical feasibility.

07 / 11
PROTOTYPE STRATEGY

DE-RISK THE FOUNDATION.

Customer validation → digital configurator → independent design/engineering feasibility → donor-based drivable mule → production-intent engineering.

08 / 11
COST DISCIPLINE

TARGETS, NOT FORECASTS.

P1 20% aspirational gross-margin goal requires ≤$14,392 cost at $17,990 delivered. Prior preliminary P1R scenario came in far too expensive for a healthy $129,990 business.

09 / 11
DISTRIBUTION

DIGITAL FIRST.

State-by-state evaluation of direct-to-consumer laws, licensed service partners, and no nonrefundable consumer reservation program before viability is demonstrated.

10 / 11
WHAT WE NEED

THE NEXT ENGINEERING MILESTONE.

A licensed or partnership-accessible donor architecture, independent validation, customer research, credible supplier quotes and a modest functional demonstrator.

11 / 11
PARTNER WITH HAVEN

BUILD WHAT OWNERS WANT TO KEEP.

Initial stage: supplier introductions, preliminary engineering opinions and milestone-based capital conversations.

DISCLOSURE

EARLY-STAGE CONCEPT

HAVEN is a proposed venture. No manufactured vehicles, validated pricing, order book, supplier contracts, or committed investment round. Illustrations are design studies; business and engineering projections are unverified. No offer of securities is made by this presentation.
