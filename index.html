04 — OpenArchitecture

Buy the car.
Not the expiration.

What if a car aged like a well-built computer? Keep the foundation. Upgrade what matters. Repair what's worn. Never throw away the whole machine for a better screen.

Modular fascia, trim, wraps and approved nonstructural panels.

Safety-certified structures and controls remain protected. Comfort, computing, appearance and validated chassis components are designed with future change in mind.

05 — Technology that respects the driver

Smart without
the gimmicks.

Front and rear recording cameras. Essential offline voice commands. Physical climate controls. A secure upgrade path for the screen and software you actually use.

Tap or hover the points. All features are engineering targets; no production system has been validated.
