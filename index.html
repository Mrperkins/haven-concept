01 / THE POINT OF HAVEN

THE NEXT
GREAT CAR
IS THE ONE
YOU KEEP.

Cars became disposable long before they stopped running. Features age. Screens lag. Parts become proprietary. Then you're expected to buy another car.

We think ownership should mean something again. HAVEN is a proposal for distinctive design, genuinely useful technology, documented repairs and upgrades that evolve with your life.
